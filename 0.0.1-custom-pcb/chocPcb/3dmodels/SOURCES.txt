Project-local component models for AdafruitNeoKeyChocSocketBreakout

Model assignments
  C1: Murata GRM155R71C104KA88D, 100 nF, 16 V, X7R, +/-10%, 0402
      (1005 metric); generic KiCad package model.
  D1: SOD-323 diode package, value 1N4148; generic package model.
  LED1: Worldsemi WS2812B-2020, conventional surface-mount 2.20 x 2.00 x
        0.84 mm package. This is a compatible replacement, not an identified
        Glove80 BOM part. Mounted on B.Cu with no PCB aperture.
        Custom simplified STEP model follows datasheet outside dimensions and
        terminals; internal chip/color details are illustrative. Reproducible
        OpenCascade source: LED_WS2812B-2020-model-source.cpp.
  SW1: Kailh CPG135001S30 Choc hotswap socket, corresponding to the Eagle
       KAILH_CHOC_SOCKET package. This models the soldered socket only.
  J1: HC-FPC-0.5-6P-FH20, the same six-signal-contact connector used in
      ../columnBreakout. Its footprint, symbol, WRL and STEP files were copied
      from that local project into EasyEDA.pretty, EasyEDA.kicad_sym and
      EasyEDA.3dshapes. LCSC part C19273925; original model files unchanged.
      Mechanical pads 7 and 8 are isolated and explicitly no-connect.

The capacitor and diode models represent packages, not newly selected procurement
part numbers. Removed JP1–JP4 headers are no longer part of this board. Their
previous generic model files remain available but are not referenced on the PCB.
All active model paths are project-relative and require no system 3D library.

Current assembly placement
  LED1 and C1 are on B.Cu, their solder pads and models on the underside.
  LED1 center: (148.5011,109.7026) mm; C1: (150.5000,109.7026) mm.
  SW1, D1 and J1 remain on F.Cu. J1 latch faces SW1 and contacts face the
  lower board edge. No LED aperture, LED keepout, or copper pour remains.
  Older reverse-mount LED and 0603 models are retained as unused assets.

Replacement references
  LED datasheet: component-docs/WS2812B-2020-V1.3.pdf (one directory above).
  https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/4684/4684_WS2812B-2020_V1.3_EN.pdf
  Pinout: 1=DOUT, 2=GND, 3=DIN, 4=VDD. Supply: 3.7 to 5.3 V, GRB data,
  800 kbit/s; reset interval >280 us. The datasheet does not require an
  external capacitor; retained 100 nF is an optional nearby bypass capacitor.
  Capacitor primary manufacturer specification:
  https://pim.murata.com/asset/pim4/ceramicCapacitorSMD/GRM155R71C104JA88-01A-EN_PDF_CERAMICCAPACITORSMD?lastModifiedDatetime=20250813144737
  MoErgo firmware identifies WS2812-compatible GRB LEDs but no exact LED or
  capacitor BOM. Checked glove80_lh.dts in moergo-sc/zmk commit
  ce69e85f585c724142aae37ddf8a7e019ff19e93.
  Replacement requires an underside optical path; light no longer passes
  through a PCB hole. These are not reverse-mount parts.

Fabrication provenance
  No JLCPCB or PCBWay identifier was found in the supplied original project.
  Eagle design rules are named "adafruit *", with two copper layers, minimum
  trace/spacing of 8 mil, minimum drill of 15.5 mil, and copper-to-edge clearance
  of 10 mil. These values do not identify a particular fabrication vendor.
