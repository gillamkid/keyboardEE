POLARITY AND PLACEMENT CHECK
  CPL rotations are the KiCad footprint rotations, normalized to 0..360 degrees.
  They are not a guarantee of JLC library/reel rotation conventions. Check the
  JLC assembly preview against the actual pad locations in panel-polarity-check.csv
  before payment, and correct library rotation offsets if the preview differs.
  For D1, its cathode stripe must sit over pad C (left on the front view).
  For LED1, pin 1 is DOUT; pin 2 GND, pin 3 DIN and pin 4 VDD.
  For J1, cable pin 1 is SWITCHA, 2 NEO_IN, 3 NEO_OUT, 4 GND, 5 VDD,
  6 SWITCHC. Pin 1 is to the right in the front view, nearest the lower edge.
  Bottom component X/Y values are in the common front/top board projection,
  NOT a mirrored camera view. Avoid manually negating bottom-side X coordinates.
  Panel origin is the lower-left corner; the same origin is used by Gerbers,
  drills and CPL. Placement X/Y are in mm; positive Y points up from that corner.
  Make sure the connector latch faces toward the switch and cable access is open.
  SW1, D1 and J1 are top; LED1 and C1 are bottom.
  TP1 is optional solder/test access on SWITCHA: no component, no paste, excluded
  from BOM/CPL. J1 anchors 7 and 8 have no attached traces and stay isolated.
  Fiducials, breakaway holes and tooling holes are not assembly components.
