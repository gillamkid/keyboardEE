Cable mapping: verify on real cables before power
Row connectors J2-J7 (1..6): row, VDD, GND, downstream LED OUT,
upstream LED IN, column. Required columnBreakout -> chocPcb pin mapping:
1->6, 2->5, 3->4, 4->3, 5->2, 6->1.
Both selected connectors are bottom-contact, 0.5 mm pitch, 0.3 mm cable thickness.
Same-side vs opposite-side FFC contacts must suit the actual board/cable geometry;
do not infer electrical mapping from the cable's advertised type alone.
Row0 LED chain entry is J1 pin10 (/IN0); final row5 return is J1 pin9 (/IN6).
J1 pins1..6 are rows0..5; pin7 VDD, pin8 GND, pin11 column, pin12 unused.
J1 anchors13/14 and row connector anchors7/8 have no attached traces.
Main-controller connector/cable compatibility was not independently verified.

Power and optional improvements
VDD and GND each have about 55.0 mm of 0.25 mm trace, all F.Cu with no vias.
Each shared rail supplies all six LED branches. For 35 um copper, approximate
end-to-end resistance is 0.108 ohm per rail / 0.217 ohm for the loop, excluding
connector/cable/pad resistance. At 0.36 A, placing all load at the far end would
produce about 0.078 V loop drop; the actual distributed six loads reduce it.
This is an estimate, not a measured result or a guaranteed allowable LED current.
Widen trunks where practical or add parallel B.Cu routing, with adequate vias,
while keeping short fanouts at connector pads. Confirm actual maximum LED draw
and connector/FFC current ratings; a ground plane is not inherently required.
