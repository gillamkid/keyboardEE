FPC layout revision, 2026-10-01

Removed JP1-JP4 (14 edge through-hole contacts), FID3/FID4 bare copper pads,
UNK_HOLE_0/1 screw holes, and obsolete header pin-label graphics. Retained the
five non-plated holes needed by the Choc switch and socket, and the reverse-LED
window. Routing vias are functional inter-layer connections, not test pads.

J1: HC-FPC-0.5-6P-FH20, copied from ../columnBreakout, mounted on F.Cu.
Origin: (143.35, 111.80) mm; rotation 180 degrees. Latch faces the switch;
six signal solder pads face the lower edge. Pin assignments:
  1 SWITCHA (column / S+)
  2 NEO_IN  (IN)
  3 NEO_OUT (OUT)
  4 GND
  5 VDD
  6 SWITCHC (row / S-)
  7: mechanical solder anchor, electrically unconnected.
  8: mechanical solder anchor, connected to GND as a copper routing waypoint.

LED1 origin (151.05,109.70) mm, rotation 0 degrees: rotated 180 degrees from
its former placement, shifted approximately 2.55 mm right. C1 is at
(155.05,109.70) mm, on the right of the LED.

Outline extents in mm:
  left:   138.9761 (unchanged)
  right:  156.1500 (formerly 158.0261)
  top:     97.2500 (formerly 92.9386)
  bottom: 114.6500 (formerly 117.0686)
  size: 17.1739 x 17.4000 mm (formerly 19.05 x 24.13 mm)
  upper corner radius: 1.27 mm (formerly 2.54)
  lower corner radius: 1.61894 mm (half the former measured radius)

The four original electrical components and their switch/diode function are
retained. Ground uses copper traces only; both GND pours have been removed, and
the schematic updated and linked to the PCB. Connector fanout is 0.127 mm wide;
other traces are 0.2032 mm wide. Routing vias are 0.64 mm with 0.35 mm drills.

Validation: zero ERC issues; zero unconnected PCB items; zero schematic parity
issues. Five ORIGINAL clearance errors remain: each LED pad is 0.123 mm from
its aperture (project requires 0.254 mm), and SW1 pad 2 reaches the unchanged
left edge. These were neither suppressed nor hidden by changing design rules.
The board therefore does not pass full fabrication DRC. See FPC-drc.rpt.

Ground trace revision, 2026-10-01
  Both PCB GND planes removed; LED cutout rule areas retained.
  GND is explicitly routed J1 pad 4 -> J1 pad 8 -> LED1 GND, with a branch
  from pad 8 to C1 GND. Copper traces enter and leave pad 8; this does not
  rely on current flowing internally through the connector's metalwork.
  Pad 8 is a side mechanical anchor, separate from the six cable contacts;
  it is not a seventh/eighth cable conductor or a duplicate of pin 1.
  Pin 1 remains SWITCHA and cable GND remains pin 4.
  The manufacturer mechanical drawing is archived in connector-docs/.
