Project-local component models for AdafruitNeoKeyChocSocketBreakout

Model assignments
  C1: 0603 (1608 metric) capacitor package, value 1uF; generic package model.
  D1: SOD-323 diode package, value 1N4148; generic package model.
  LED1: SK6812MINI-E reverse-mount LED package, corresponding to the Eagle
        WS2812B_SK6812E / NEO3535_REVERSE part. Emitting face points through
        the PCB cutout toward the opposite side.
  SW1: Kailh CPG135001S30 Choc hotswap socket, corresponding to the Eagle
       KAILH_CHOC_SOCKET package. This models the soldered socket only.
  JP1, JP3: optional 1x05, 2.54 mm straight male headers.
  JP2, JP4: optional 1x02, 2.54 mm straight male headers.
  Fiducials, artwork, labels and mounting holes do not receive component models.

The capacitor, diode and headers are package representations, not newly selected
procurement part numbers. Existing values, pin numbering and electrical geometry
are retained. Through-hole headers may be omitted in a wire-connected assembly.

All model references use ${KIPRJMOD}/3dmodels so the project is portable and does
not require a system-wide 3D library or the impartGUI plugin. Matching model
references are also stored in the six project footprint-library entries.

Eagle import side correction
  On the imported board, LED1 and SW1 were marked B.Cu while their SMD solder
  pads were on F.Cu. Their footprint side labels are now F.Cu. The corresponding
  source footprint-library entries are marked B.Cu, matching their native SMD
  pads. Pads, nets, tracks, holes and other footprint geometry are unchanged.
  Library-to-board flipping gives the existing F.Cu assembly. The assembly-side
  render matches the original Adafruit product photograph in assets/5756.jpg.

Fabrication provenance
  No JLCPCB or PCBWay identifier was found in the supplied original project.
  Eagle design rules are named "adafruit *", with two copper layers, minimum
  trace/spacing of 8 mil, minimum drill of 15.5 mil, and copper-to-edge clearance
  of 10 mil. These values do not identify a particular fabrication vendor.

Validation
  All eight solderable component footprints have a resolvable project-local
  STEP model. Both board sides were rendered, and a complete STEP assembly was
  exported successfully. The original board and footprint files were backed up
  in AdafruitNeoKeyChocSocketBreakout-backups/before-3d-models-*.zip.
